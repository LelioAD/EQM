<span class="eqm-eyebrow">The Collective · for stewards · since 2018</span>

<p class="eqm-lead">Stewards lead the Collective forward, calmly and together. Bring your gifts; we'll find the seat where they shine.</p>

<p>The Collective began as a spiritual idea: every person carries something the whole needs. In 2018 it became a gathering of people. Now it has the tools to build.</p>

<div class="eqm-panel">
  <h3>What you get as a steward</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>A seat on the team, in the role that fits your gifts.</li>
    <li>Your contribution recorded as sweat equity from day one.</li>
    <li>Calm, respectful work where ideas meet and the better one wins.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

## Four ways in

<div class="eqm-grid">
  <div><b>Creators</b>Publish and promote your work on the platform.</div>
  <div><b>Makers</b>Produce your own goods, from soap to art.</div>
  <div><b>Backers</b>Help fund the work you believe in.</div>
  <div><b>Stewards</b>Build the company with us, day to day.</div>
</div>

Be one, or be several. Many creators are makers and backers too.
